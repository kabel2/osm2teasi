Teasi map installer

Puts a map from OpenStreetMap onto a Teasi PRO straight from the browser: it deletes the old map of that country, writes the new files while they download, and fixes the entries in BikeNav/packages.xml so the device does not complain at startup.

This browser cannot write to a drive. Use Chrome, Edge or another Chromium-based browser on a computer (not on a phone).

1. Connect the Teasi

Connect it by USB, confirm the connection on its display, then choose the Teasi drive (or its BikeNav folder) and allow editing.

Downloads share 10 Mbit/s, so a big country takes a while (Germany, 1.5 GB: about 20 minutes). Keep this tab open until it says done, then eject the drive before you unplug the Teasi.
